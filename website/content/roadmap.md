# Roadmap

Things we might add to Vertex. **These are ideas, not promises - nothing here has a date**, and
the order can change. What already exists is on the [Features](/features) page, and what has just
shipped is on the [Changelog](/changelog).

## Around the app

- **A better Home page** - pick up where you left off, jump into a quick game, see which friends are
  online, your daily reward and quest progress, and what's new, all in one place.
- **Stats inside your Profile** - a full page of your plays, wins and ratings for every game.
- **A tidier sidebar** - grouped, scrollable, and easier to find things in as Vertex grows.
- **A refreshed logo.**

## Playing together

- **Rejoining the group games** - reconnecting after a dropped connection already works in the
  two-player games; getting back into a party game like Among Us or Racing is a different problem we
  want to look at.
- **Chat in more games** - match chat is in 14 online games and Telephone; more of them could have it.
- **More on the Forums** - editing your own posts, votes, images and notifications.

## New things to play

- **Vertex: Dominion** - a persistent nation game about diplomacy, trade and war, checked in on a
  little each day rather than played in one sitting.
- **More original games**, including more real-time and party games.

## Servers

- **Servers you can join from anywhere** - Vertex is self-hosted today; we are looking at running
  servers that anyone can reach without being on the same network as a friend.
